    Voiced and combos

    ゛ adds voicing (か→が), ゜ makes p (は→ぱ). Small ゃゅょ combine (き+ゃ=きゃ kya). Small っ doubles the next consonant (きって kitte). Long vowels: おかあさん, katakana uses ー (コーヒー).

    Verb groups

    Group 1 (u-verbs): ending changes vowel. Group 2 (ru-verbs): drop る. Irregular: する, くる. Watch: かえる, はいる, はしる, しる, きる(cut) are group 1.
